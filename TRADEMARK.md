# Khaoyu Note trademark policy

The Khaoyu Note name, wordmark, logo and app icon are brand identifiers. The AGPL-3.0-only license
covers the source code, not those brand assets.

## Forks and commercial distributions

You may fork, modify and distribute the code, including commercially, under the AGPL. If you
distribute a modified version or run it as a network service, the AGPL requires you to keep the
complete corresponding source available under the same license, preserve copyright and license
notices, and give proper attribution.

A fork or paid distribution must not use the Khaoyu Note name, logo or app icon in a way that suggests
it is the official Khaoyu Note app. Replace the name, logo, app icon, screenshots and branding before
publishing a fork. Accurate statements such as "based on Khaoyu Note" are allowed when they do not
imply endorsement.

## Not permitted

- Publishing a modified build under the Khaoyu Note name or logo.
- Using the Khaoyu Note app icon for a fork or third-party distribution.
- Claiming official status, affiliation or endorsement without written permission.

## Attribution

Keep the copyright notice and `SPDX-License-Identifier: AGPL-3.0-only` in the source, and link to the
corresponding source from any network-facing fork.

This policy is a trademark rule, not an extra restriction on the rights granted by the AGPL.
