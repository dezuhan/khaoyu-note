Service admin help

The dashboard manages this self-hosted Khaoyu Note service.

  • Overview — account, team and activity counts.
  • Accounts — search accounts, change role or status.
  • Teams — create teams and inspect members.
  • Activity — audit trail with filters.
  • Sharing — public sharing and scoped share links.
  • Account settings — profile, password, sessions and API tokens.

Press Ctrl K to search commands. The Account menu is in the top bar.
