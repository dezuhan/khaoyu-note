The dashboard manages this self-hosted Khaoyu Note service.

  • Overview — account, board and activity counts.
  • Accounts — create members, search accounts, change role or status.
  • Boards — create, rename and delete boards, and add, update or remove members.
  • Activity — audit trail with filters.
  • Sharing — public sharing and scoped share links.
  • Account Management — Profile, Security (password, 2FA, passkeys) and Login sessions.

Press Ctrl K to search commands. The Account menu is in the top bar.
